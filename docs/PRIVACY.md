# PhotoRank privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-25_

PhotoRank is a game that helps you rank the photos already on your phone.

## What the app accesses
- **Photos on your device.** With your permission, PhotoRank reads the photo
  library on your phone (through the system media store) to show you your own
  photos and to store, per photo, a rating and the decisions you made about it.

## What the app stores
- Ratings, decisions, session history and settings are stored **only on your
  device**, in the app's private storage.
- Thumbnails shown in the home-screen widget are rendered to the app's private
  storage on your device.

## What leaves your device
- **Nothing, unless you choose to share.** PhotoRank has no server, no
  account, no analytics and no advertising SDK. It does not upload photos,
  ratings or usage data anywhere.
- When you tap **Share**, the app hands an image you generated (for example a
  "Top 9" grid) to the system share sheet. Where it goes from there is your
  choice.
- "Mark as favourites" and "Export album" write to your phone's own photo
  library, on your device.

## Arena (optional, online)
Arena is an opt-in daily game. Nothing below happens unless you enter a photo.
- **What is uploaded:** only the single photo you choose for that day, reduced
  to at most 1280 px on its long edge, with all metadata (location, camera,
  time) removed. It is stored on our server (hosted by Supabase) so other
  players can rate it and it can appear on that day's leaderboard.
- **Identity:** you get an anonymous account tied to your device. You may
  optionally choose a username; it is shown next to your photo.
- **What others see:** your entry photo, your username (or "Anonymous"), and
  its rank. Your other photos, ratings and library are never uploaded.
- **What we record:** your entries, the duels you rate (which of two photos
  you picked), rooms you join, follows, blocks and reports.
- **Deletion:** withdraw any entry from the app and the photo is deleted
  from the server. Uninstalling stops the account being used; contact us to
  delete the account and its history.
- **Notifications (optional):** if you enable result notifications, the app
  registers a push token for your device with our server so it can tell you
  where your photo finished. The token identifies the device only and is
  deleted when it stops working or when your account is deleted.
- **Moderation:** entries can be reported and blocked in the app. Reported
  photos are hidden pending review; accounts posting abusive or illegal
  content are banned. You must be 13 or older to use Arena.

## Photos are never deleted
PhotoRank never deletes, moves or hides your photos. Deleting the app deletes
all of its data.

## Permissions
- Photos and videos (read): to show and rank your photos.
- Notifications (optional): only if you turn on reminders in Settings.

## Children
PhotoRank is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data.
