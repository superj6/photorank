Delete your PhotoRank data or account

PhotoRank's online features (the Arena and friends' sets) use an account created in the app. You can delete individual data, or the whole account, yourself in the app at any time.

Delete some of your data (keep the account)

  • An arena entry: open your entry card in the Arena tab and withdraw it — the photo and its results are deleted from our servers immediately.
  • Your published set: Friends → your set's menu → Unpublish — all its photos and every friend's rankings of them are deleted immediately.

Delete the whole account

  1. Open PhotoRank → Settings.
  2. Under your username, tap Delete arena account and confirm.

This permanently removes the account and everything associated with it from our servers: uploaded photos, arena entries and results, published sets and friends' rankings of them, follows, and the account record itself. It takes effect immediately. Photo rankings stored on your device are yours and are not touched.

To request deletion of some or all of your data without using the app — or if you can no longer open it — email super_j@att.net from any address, including your arena username, and we will delete the requested data (or the whole account) within 30 days.
